Simply Supported Beam — Theory

System

A simply supported beam is supported by a pin at A (fixed in x and y) and a roller at B (fixed in y only). It is statically determinate with 3 equilibrium equations.

Equilibrium — Reactions

ΣF_y = 0 → A + B = ΣF_i + ΣqᵢLᵢ
ΣM_A = 0 → B = ( ΣFᵢ·aᵢ + Σqᵢ·Lᵢ·x̄ᵢ ) / L
x̄ᵢ = centroid of distributed load i

Shear Force Q(x)

The shear force at position x is the sum of all vertical forces to the left of the section:

Q(x) = A − Σ Fᵢ · H(x − aᵢ) − Σ qᵢ · ⟨x − aᵢ⟩
H = Heaviside step function

Bending Moment M(x)

The bending moment is the integral of the shear force. Tension-bottom convention: positive M shown downward.

M(x) = A·x − Σ Fᵢ·(x−aᵢ)·H(x−aᵢ)
− Σ qᵢ·⟨x−aᵢ⟩²/2

Inclined Point Loads

An inclined load F at angle α (from vertical) is decomposed into components:

F_y = F · cos(α) ← vertical (contributes to Q, M)
F_x = F · sin(α) ← horizontal (contributes to N)
Cantilever — Theory

System

A cantilever is fixed at one end (built-in support at A) and free at the other. The fixed support provides a vertical reaction V_A, a horizontal reaction H_A, and a restraining moment M_A.

Reactions at Support A

V_A = Σ Fᵢ·cos(αᵢ) + Σ qᵢ·Lᵢ
H_A = Σ Fᵢ·sin(αᵢ)
M_A = Σ Fᵢ·cos(αᵢ)·aᵢ + Σ qᵢ·Lᵢ·x̄ᵢ
x̄ᵢ = centroid of distributed load segment

Shear Force Q(x)

Computed from the fixed support (x = 0) toward the free end:

Q(x) = V_A − Σ Fᵢ·cos(αᵢ)·H(x−aᵢ) − Σ qᵢ·⟨x−aᵢ⟩

Bending Moment M(x)

Maximum at the fixed support (x = 0), zero at the free end. Tension on top.

M(x) = −M_A + V_A·x − Σ Fᵢ·cos(αᵢ)·(x−aᵢ)·H(x−aᵢ)
− Σ qᵢ·⟨x−aᵢ⟩²/2
